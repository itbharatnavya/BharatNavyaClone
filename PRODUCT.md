# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indian startup founders and MSME operators looking for credible suppliers, experts, and service providers; and businesses that want to establish a trusted digital presence and receive qualified inquiries.

## Product Purpose

BharatConnect turns an imprecise business need into a structured brief, explains which businesses fit, and helps both sides begin a useful conversation. Phase one succeeds when founders can discover and contact a relevant business and providers can publish an evidence-backed profile without mistaking the platform for e-commerce.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, energetic, professional. Clear and capable rather than institutional; contemporary without decorative cultural shorthand.

## Anti-references

Not IndiaMART's visual density, a generic KPI dashboard, a chatbot-led AI product, a beige startup template, or a card-heavy marketplace. Avoid gradients, glass panels, bento grids, cultural motifs, opaque AI scores, and decorative motion.

## Design Principles

- Start with the need, not the category tree.
- Make evidence easier to compare than marketing claims.
- Explain matches and gaps without false precision.
- Keep one account and one shared business context across both sides.
- Reveal complexity progressively, especially on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, complete keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, 44px minimum targets, 200% zoom resilience, semantic forms, and translation-ready layouts.
