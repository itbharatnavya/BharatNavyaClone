# Direction 02 — Living Business Network

## The thinking

This direction makes Bharat Navya feel current and alive. Instead of presenting only static cards, the homepage shows useful public activity: completed work, company announcements, verified review excerpts, and newly opened roles.

The central question is: **What are credible businesses doing right now, and what opportunities does that activity create?** Activity improves discovery, but stable company profiles and job records remain the source of truth. The concept deliberately avoids becoming a social network.

## Visual direction

- More energetic composition with Plum Ink, Iris, Coral, Aqua, and Lavender Mist.
- Strong workplace photography and selected activity panels.
- Sora-like display typography, readable Noto Sans-like body copy, and compact mono metadata.
- Alternating section rhythm rather than a uniform card catalogue.
- Finite, curated activity modules instead of an infinite feed.

## Important features

- Universal search with equal Directory and Recruitment gateways.
- A selected company-activity stage showing completed work, an update, a review, or a current role.
- Company profiles with capabilities, selected work, proof, verified reviews, updates, and hiring information.
- Current-role modules connected to employer activity.
- Related guides that help users interpret company evidence or job context.
- Structured inquiry card on the company profile.
- Moderated public updates with clear dates and company attribution.

## AI role

AI can summarise a finite set of public updates and suggest related company profiles, roles, or guides. Summaries must retain source links and clearly indicate when information is incomplete.

AI does not invent activity, expose private actions, decide which company is trustworthy, or create an engagement-maximising feed.

## Advertising approach

A restrained sponsor rail or card is separated from public activity, verification, reviews, and inquiry controls. Sponsored content is never styled as a company update.

## Why choose this direction

- Gives Bharat Navya a distinctive sense of freshness.
- Encourages repeat visits without relying on social-media mechanics.
- Lets companies demonstrate momentum through real work and hiring.
- Connects Directory and Recruitment naturally through company activity.

## Risks to manage

- Requires moderation and clear rules for what qualifies as a public update.
- Sparse activity could make the platform appear inactive during early growth.
- The interface must avoid reactions, follower counts, vanity metrics, and infinite scrolling.
