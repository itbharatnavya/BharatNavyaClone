# Direction 03 — Editorial Exchange

## The thinking

This direction positions Bharat Navya as both a trusted business platform and a useful publication. Stories and guides are not generic SEO content: every piece helps somebody evaluate a company, understand an industry, prepare an inquiry, assess an employer, or apply for a role.

The central question is: **Can useful reading create a better path into company and job discovery?** Editorial content creates context and authority, while Directory and Recruitment remain the places where users act.

## Visual direction

- Petrol, pale Sky, Ochre, Berry, Ink, and white.
- Contrasting Source Serif-like editorial headlines and Archivo-like interface typography.
- Large original workplace imagery, fine rules, and asymmetric editorial modules.
- A measured publication rhythm without imitating a newspaper front page.
- Clear visual separation between editorial, records, and sponsored features.

## Important features

- Lead business story linked to relevant companies or roles.
- Universal search and equal Directory and Recruitment gateways above the fold.
- Featured company records and current roles alongside practical guides.
- Author, date, topic, related companies, and related jobs on editorial content.
- Recruitment results with conventional filters and transparent job metadata.
- Related career-guide rail supporting the job-search experience.
- Concise public company updates for recency.

## AI role

AI produces clearly labelled, source-led article summaries and connects reading to relevant Directory or Recruitment records. The selected concept explicitly labels summaries as illustrative and tells users to review sources.

AI does not generate live market claims without evidence, replace the article author, or turn the page into a chat experience.

## Advertising approach

Sponsored features use a visibly different background, rule, and label. A sponsor cannot inherit editorial authority, employer verification, or organic search position.

## Why choose this direction

- Creates a premium and authoritative Bharat Navya identity.
- Gives blogs a clear product purpose instead of treating them as filler.
- Supports education-heavy categories where users need context before acting.
- Provides strong surfaces for brand storytelling and carefully controlled sponsorship.

## Risks to manage

- Editorial production requires consistent quality, authorship, and fact-checking.
- The homepage could drift into a media site if search and gateways lose prominence.
- AI summaries need strong source and freshness labelling.
