# Bharat Navya Concept Options

This folder is the presentation-ready handoff for the Bharat Navya visual exploration. It contains four materially different ways to express the same product foundation:

- **Home** introduces the platform and gives equal importance to Directory and Recruitment.
- **Directory** helps people discover companies, examine proof and reviews, and send inquiries.
- **Recruitment** helps people find current roles, understand employers, and apply.

The current Bharat Navya implementation remains the control direction and is not included as one of these four options.

## Options at a glance

| Option | Central idea | Best suited for | Signature feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| [01 — Proof-led Marketplace](./01-proof-led-marketplace/README.md) | Make trust and evidence the centre of discovery | A clear, broadly usable marketplace direction | Media-rich company cards with proof and transparent match reasons |
| [02 — Living Business Network](./02-living-business-network/README.md) | Show that companies and opportunities are active now | A more energetic and differentiated platform | Finite company-activity stage connected to stable records |
| [03 — Editorial Exchange](./03-editorial-exchange/README.md) | Combine useful business reading with actions | A more premium, authoritative brand position | Publication-quality stories linked to companies and jobs |
| [04 — Smart Platform Navigator](./04-smart-platform-navigator/README.md) | Make search more conversational without becoming a chatbot | A product-led, AI-assisted discovery direction | Natural language translated into visible editable filters |

## Shared product and trust rules

- Directory and Recruitment remain visible as equal product pillars.
- AI assists search, explanation, summarisation, or drafting; it never becomes the product itself.
- Users can inspect and change AI-interpreted filters.
- AI never fabricates verification, silently ranks companies, automatically contacts businesses, or automatically submits applications.
- Blogs and guides support real company-selection or job-search decisions.
- Public activity may include company updates, completed work, reviews, and hiring announcements. Private actions never appear publicly.
- Paid placements are labelled **Sponsored**, visually separated from organic records, and excluded from verification, ranking, inquiries, and applications.
- All companies and marks shown in these concepts are fictional.

## Folder structure

Each direction folder contains:

1. A desktop homepage.
2. A direction-specific desktop product screen.
3. A mobile homepage.
4. A `README.md` explaining the thinking, features, visual language, AI behaviour, advertising treatment, strengths, risks, and recommended use.

Earlier source assets remain in `../assets/`. These presentation copies are intentionally separate so the direction folders can be shared as a self-contained package.
