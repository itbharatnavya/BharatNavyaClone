# Bharat Navya V2 — First-glance Visual Calibrations

These are presentation-only desktop homepage experiments for Mahir. They do not modify or specify production frontend code. Existing V1 concepts remain unchanged.

## Purpose

The V1 concepts expressed four different product theses but converged visually around the same structure: white page, large headline, universal search, two gateway cards, and dense banks of company, role, guide, activity, and sponsor cards.

V2 tests whether the four theses can be recognised immediately without reading their direction names.

## Shared product foundation

- Navigation remains Home, Directory, and Recruitment.
- Directory covers company discovery, profiles, proof, reviews, and inquiries.
- Recruitment covers job discovery, employer context, role details, and applications.
- AI, editorial content, activity, and sponsorship remain supporting systems.
- No catalogue, commerce, RFQ, finance, lending, consultations, candidate marketplace, or Growth OS patterns.
- The supplied Bharat Navya mark remains unchanged and is not expanded into decorative tricolour styling.

## V2 rules

1. Do not use the same headline/search/two-card hero composition more than once.
2. Each direction gets one dominant visual device and removes modules that weaken it.
3. Company and role content may be shared for comparison, but its visual treatment must differ.
4. Sponsorship is shown only when it helps demonstrate the direction and must remain clearly separate.
5. Generated workplace imagery must vary by art direction rather than reuse the same pristine factory scene.
6. If the screenshots are blurred, converted to grayscale, or stripped of labels, their silhouettes should still differ.

## Direction 01 prompt sheet — Proof-led Marketplace

**Revised visual thesis:** Bharat Navya as a calm, human marketplace where evidence is immediately visible but never overwhelms discovery.

- A bold navy plane and one dominant documentary workplace image create the first-glance identity.
- The landing viewport has one message, one search control, and no competing module grid.
- Directory and Recruitment remain obvious through two quiet text routes rather than oversized gateway cards.
- AI appears only as a short promise that the query will become editable filters; no chatbot or generated brief is shown.
- Proof is compressed into one company strip with three signals: identity, recent work, and verified reviews.
- Detailed methodology, reviews, jobs, guides, activity, and sponsorship are progressively disclosed below the first viewport.
- Maximum meaningful landing actions: Search, Browse Directory, and Find current roles.
- The V2 registry/table concept is retained only as rejected design evidence and must not guide subsequent Direction 01 screens.

## Direction 02 prompt sheet — Living Business Network

**Visual thesis:** Bharat Navya as a current, human view of businesses at work.

- Committed plum field with coral and aqua signals; substantially more colour than the other directions.
- Large candid activity photograph dominates the canvas.
- A visible seven-day time rail and a short stack of activity selectors.
- Directory and Recruitment appear as actions attached to the selected activity and in the header—not generic gateway cards.
- Few conventional cards; use borderless media blocks and editorial captions.
- No likes, followers, reactions, comments, or endless feed.

## Direction 03 prompt sheet — Editorial Exchange

**Visual thesis:** Bharat Navya as a spacious business publication connected to action.

- Pale blue publication canvas, petrol ink, berry and ochre accents.
- One decisive documentary photograph and one lead story; generous negative space.
- Large editorial serif headline contrasted with compact sans navigation and metadata.
- Directory and Recruitment are typographic routes integrated into the editorial spread.
- Guides, companies, and roles appear as an edited selection with captions and rules—not card banks.
- Avoid dense broadsheet imitation and avoid filling the viewport with every platform module.

## Direction 04 prompt sheet — Smart Platform Navigator

**Visual thesis:** Bharat Navya as a transparent intent-to-result search product.

- Graphite search canvas occupying most of the viewport with electric blue and sea-green controls.
- Search query, visible interpretation, and one focused split result preview form the entire hero.
- Directory and Recruitment use a segmented mode control instead of gateway cards.
- Results are compact rows and evidence disclosures, not image-heavy marketplace cards.
- Minimal photography; only small record thumbnails where evidence benefits.
- No chatbot persona, chat bubbles, dashboard analytics, autonomous actions, or operating-system metaphor.

## Review gate

These four screens should be reviewed side by side before any V2 scrolling pages, Directory pages, Recruitment pages, or mobile adaptations are generated. A direction should proceed only if its first-glance identity and interaction thesis are both clear.
