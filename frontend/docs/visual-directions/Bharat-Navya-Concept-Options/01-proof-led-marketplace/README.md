# Direction 01 — Proof-led Marketplace

## The thinking

This direction treats Bharat Navya as a polished marketplace where credibility is visible before a user opens a full company or job record. It is the most direct evolution of the existing platform because it keeps familiar search, category, company-card, job-card, review, and inquiry patterns while improving their hierarchy and visual quality.

The central question is: **How can a user quickly decide which company or opportunity deserves a closer look?** Photography creates interest, but proof, capabilities, location, reviews, recent work, and employer context create confidence.

## Visual direction

- Professional marketplace composition with a strong grid and generous white space.
- Rich workplace and project imagery used as evidence, not decoration.
- Clear typography and restrained colour accents.
- Cards carry meaningful facts instead of decorative statistics.
- Directory and Recruitment appear as equal gateways immediately after universal search.

## Important features

- Universal search across companies, services, and jobs.
- Prominent Directory and Recruitment gateway cards.
- Featured verified companies with capabilities, location, review context, and recent work.
- Current roles with employer, workplace, salary, and freshness information.
- Directory filters for category, location, and verification.
- Shortlisting and lightweight company comparison.
- Practical guides connected to inquiries, company evaluation, and applications.
- Public company updates that resolve to stable profiles.

## AI role

AI quietly interprets a natural-language search and turns it into visible filters. A small **Why this result** disclosure explains matches using facts already visible on the record. Users can edit the filters, and no unexplained score is shown.

AI does not contact a company, submit an inquiry, rank sponsors as organic results, or create verification claims.

## Advertising approach

Sponsored placements are visually distinct and explicitly labelled. They do not receive verified styling and sit outside the organic comparison order.

## Why choose this direction

- Easiest for new users to understand.
- Strong balance between visual appeal and marketplace utility.
- Closest to the current product model, reducing implementation risk.
- Makes trust, reviews, and proof useful without feeling institutional.

## Risks to manage

- Too much imagery could reduce information density on smaller screens.
- Verification and review labels need strict real-data rules in production.
- Comparison should remain lightweight and not drift into procurement or RFQ workflows.
